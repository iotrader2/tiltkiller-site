Trade with NO emotions

Rules in. Gut out.

Every alert follows the same written rules. That keeps the plan steady when the market isn’t.

  • Same written rules on every alert — no rewriting the plan mid-trade
  • A set stop on every trade — risk decided before you click
  • Max 3 new trades a day — so you know your risk up front
  • No gut calls. No revenge trades. No FOMO.

For newer traders: keep it simple. Following a tested, rules-based system can save you years of trial and error learning how not to blow up an account — and how to get consistent. It doesn’t guarantee profits.

How it works

No guesswork. A clear alert, a clear stop, and a size that matches what you're willing to lose.

1Get the alert in Discord. When a setup is ready, you see the name, the entry, and where the stop goes.
2Know the plan before you click. Entry and stop are decided up front — not after the trade starts moving.
3Size it to your risk. You pick how much of your account you're willing to lose if the stop hits. TiltKiller keeps that number honest.

Alerts in Discord. That's the product.

Alerts hit Discord in the first hour after the opening bell. We check every 15-minute candle from the open to 10:45 ET — so you know your trade, entry, and stop early, then get on with your day.

  • 1
    Alert arrives. The stock, the entry price, and the stop — in one Discord message, before 10:45 ET.
  • 2
    You size the trade. Decide how much you're okay losing if you're wrong. That amount is your 1R (the money at risk on that trade).
  • 3
    You follow the rules. Stop goes in right away. No revenge trades. No guessing mid-raid.
