How the alerts work

No guesswork. Buy alerts hit Discord in the first hour after the open — every 15-minute candle through 10:45 ET — with entry and stop. Sell alerts fire when it’s time. Size it to what you're willing to lose.

1Get the buy alert in Discord. You get the buy alert with entry and stop — the name, where to get in, and where the stop goes.
2Size it to your risk. You pick how much of your account you're willing to lose if the stop hits. The alert keeps that number clear.
3Get the sell alert when it’s time. Winners are held with a trailing stop, and you get an alert when it’s hit — so you know when to get out.

Trade with NO emotions

Rules in. Gut out.

Every High Growth Leaders alert follows the same written rules. That keeps the plan steady when the market isn’t.

  • Alerts for the way in and the way out — buy and sell, same written rules
  • A set stop on every trade — risk decided before you click
  • Max 3 new trades a day — so you know your risk up front
  • No gut calls. No revenge trades. No FOMO.

For newer traders: keep it simple. Following a tested, rules-based system can save you years of trial and error learning how not to blow up an account — and how to get consistent. It doesn’t guarantee profits.
