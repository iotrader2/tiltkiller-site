How it works

No guesswork. Alerts for the way in and the way out — then a size that matches what you're willing to lose.

1Get the buy alert in Discord. You get the buy alert with entry and stop — the name, where to get in, and where the stop goes.
2Size it to your risk. You pick how much of your account you're willing to lose if the stop hits. TiltKiller keeps that number honest.
3Get the sell alert when it’s time. Winners are held with a trailing stop, and you get an alert when it’s hit — so you know when to get out.
