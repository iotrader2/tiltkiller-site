TrendSpider alternatives for swing traders

TrendSpider is a full trading platform with a no-code Strategy Tester. If you mainly want to build and backtest a stock strategy without writing code, stratBacktest is a focused alternative — not a replacement for the rest of TrendSpider.

This comparison is current as of November 2026. Features and plans change; the official TrendSpider and TiltKiller pages are the source of truth.

People searching for a TrendSpider alternative are often trying to backtest a strategy without writing code, especially swing traders who work in stocks. That is a fair question, and it is also easy to over-claim. TrendSpider already has a no-code Strategy Tester. stratBacktest is an alternative if you want a narrower tool aimed at stock strategies, not because TrendSpider “can’t” backtest without code.

Backtesting a stock strategy without coding

You have more than one honest option:

  • TrendSpider’s Strategy Tester. A visual editor for entry, exit and risk rules. You can also start from a template, import a strategy, or ask Sidekick, TrendSpider’s built-in assistant, to help. Sidekick is optional. You can inspect and edit the rules either way.
  • TradingView’s built-in and community strategies. You can add those to a chart without writing code. Your own custom rules need Pine Script.
  • stratBacktest. A seven-step form for a stock strategy, then a backtest across a universe of US stocks and ETFs. No script.

Spreadsheets can help you review trades you already have, but they are a poor way to replay entries and exits across a whole market. None of these tools can promise future profits. A backtest is a simulation of the past.

What TrendSpider is, fairly

TrendSpider describes itself as a professional market terminal: idea generation, research, strategy development, and timing and execution in one place. The Strategy Tester is one part of that. You define entry, exit and risk conditions, then see trade-by-trade results, an equity curve, drawdown, and related metrics. You can account for estimated trading costs, inspect individual trades, and compare variations.

Two capabilities stand out if you are comparing platforms. First, history: TrendSpider says you can backtest with 50+ years of data depending on the symbol and the feed, often back to when the name started trading. Second, breadth: the Group Strategy Tester runs the same idea across many symbols, timeframes and history windows, and TrendSpider’s product page cites up to 500 symbols. Higher plans also unlock finer backtest timeframes, which matters more for short-term trading than for daily swing work.

TrendSpider is also honest about what a backtest cannot do. Its help pages say a tester is better at showing that a strategy is weak than at proving it is good, that past results do not guarantee future ones, and that the simulation assumes you can get the modeled fill. Those limits apply to stratBacktest too.

Where stratBacktest is a real alternative

If your question is “I swing-trade US stocks and I want to write a strategy, test it without code, and see what it would have done,” stratBacktest is built for that job and little else.

  • A seven-step builder: universe, market filter, setups, entry, stop, exits, and limits and size.
  • Three years of daily and 15-minute data, across liquid US stocks and ETFs or TiltKiller’s 662 theme stocks.
  • Side-by-side tests of one setting at a time, with total R and drawdown for each.
  • Every trade on a marked-up chart, with suggested rule changes from the trades you flag.
  • An account curve at a risk per trade you choose, including progressive exposure: raising risk as recent results improve and lowering it as they worsen, with examples that top out at 0.5% per trade. The idea is closely associated with trader Mark Minervini, and stratBacktest’s version is modeled on his approach. TiltKiller isn’t affiliated with him.

What you give up, relative to TrendSpider, is the rest of the terminal: long history, many markets, scans, bots, broker routing, and an assistant that can build strategies for you. stratBacktest is in beta and invite-only. It is not a charting platform.

Who should use which

Stay with TrendSpider, or start there, if you

  • Want one platform for charts, scans, alerts and bots, not only a backtest.
  • Trade futures, forex or crypto as well as stocks, or want 50+ years of history where the data exists.
  • Want to compare a strategy across many symbols and timeframes in one grid.
  • Want to turn a tested strategy into alerts or a Strategy Bot, or to use Sidekick while you work.

Try stratBacktest if you

  • Are a swing trader focused on US stocks and want a no-code builder aimed at that.
  • Want a universe-wide test on daily and 15-minute data, and to compare one setting at a time in R.
  • Want to walk every trade on a chart and test progressive exposure on the account.
  • Do not need the rest of a market terminal in the same product.

Some traders will keep TrendSpider for research and execution and use stratBacktest for a second look at a stock strategy. That is a reasonable split. Neither product can guarantee results, and a strong backtest can still fail in live trading.

Common questions

Is stratBacktest a TrendSpider alternative?

It can be, if you mainly want to build and backtest a stock strategy without writing code. It is not a replacement for TrendSpider as a whole. TrendSpider is an all-in-one platform with charts, scans, alerts, trading bots, and tools for several markets.

Do I need to write code to backtest on TrendSpider?

No. TrendSpider’s Strategy Tester has a visual editor for entry, exit, and risk rules. You can also start from a template or ask Sidekick, its built-in assistant, to help. Sidekick is optional.

Why would a swing trader try stratBacktest instead of TrendSpider?

If you trade US stocks, want a seven-step builder, want to run the same rules across a universe of names on daily and 15-minute data, compare one setting at a time in R, review every trade, or test progressive exposure. If you want a full research terminal, many years of history, or bots and scans in the same product, TrendSpider is built for that.

Does stratBacktest have scanning, alerts, and trading bots?

No. stratBacktest is a backtester. stratAlerts, TiltKiller’s rules-based buy and sell alerts in Discord, is coming soon. It is a separate product.

Is stratBacktest free?

stratBacktest is in beta and invite-only. It is not offered as a free public tool. TrendSpider is a paid subscription platform with several plans; see TrendSpider’s pricing page for current rates.
