Privacy Policy

What information we collect, how we use it, and how you can ask us to delete it.

Who we are

TiltKiller is operated by Innovative Objects LLC, an Arizona limited liability company, doing business as TiltKiller. You can reach us at tiltkillerapp@gmail.com.

Our products are:

  • stratAlerts, a strategy trade alerts service. Status: Coming soon.
  • stratBacktest, a backtesting tool. Status: Coming soon / private beta.

Information we collect

If you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you submit. Waitlist signups are handled by FormSubmit.

If you create a stratBacktest account, we collect your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name and profile photo. We only request the Google permissions we need to sign you in: openid, email, and profile.

We email sign-in codes through Resend. Account sign-in is handled by Neon Auth. App data you create in stratBacktest — such as saved strategies, drafts, backtest runs, and settings — is stored in a Neon Postgres database.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The stratBacktest app is hosted on Vercel, and the backtest API runs on a Cloudflare worker.

How we use your information

We use this information only to run the service and to contact you about it. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for ads.

Google user data is used only to sign you in. TiltKiller’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data we hold about you by emailing tiltkillerapp@gmail.com.
