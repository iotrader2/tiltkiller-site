Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, who handles it, and how to have it deleted.

Who we are

TiltKiller is operated by Innovative Objects LLC, an Arizona limited liability company, doing business as TiltKiller. You can reach us at tiltkillerapp@gmail.com.

This policy covers tiltkiller.com and our products:

  • stratAlerts, a strategy trade alerts service (coming soon).
  • stratBacktest, a strategy backtesting app, in an invite-only beta.

Information we collect

Waitlist. If you join a waitlist on this site, we collect the email address you submit. Waitlist sign-ups are handled by FormSubmit.

Your stratBacktest account. You can sign in with an emailed code or with Google. Either way, we receive your email address and the name on your account. With Google, we also receive your profile photo; we don't show or use it. We ask Google only for the permissions needed to sign you in: openid, email, and profile. We never get your Google password, and we can't read your Gmail, contacts, or files.

Asking for beta access. If you sign in before you're invited, you can ask to be let in. We keep your name, email, the messages you write (your 20 most recent, up to 500 characters each), how many times you asked, and when. We keep this even if we say "not now", so we can see interest and let you in later.

What you build. The strategies you create and save, their versions, and which version you tagged are stored with your account. Admins of TiltKiller can see every strategy saved in stratBacktest, to run and support the service. Other users can't see yours.

Kept in your browser. Some things stay only in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us: unsaved drafts, trades you flag for review, the account value you enter on the Impact on Account tab, and similar display settings. Signing in also stores a session cookie so you stay signed in.

Usage and logs. Our hosting providers record standard request logs, such as IP address, browser type, and the time of a request, to keep the service running and secure. We don't use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

How we use your information

We use this information only to run stratBacktest: to sign you in, decide who has beta access, save and run your strategies, and contact you about the service (for example, sign-in codes or letting you know you've been invited). We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for ads.

Google user data is used only to sign you in. TiltKiller’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who handles it for us

We use these providers to run the service. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Neon: account sign-in (Neon Auth) and the database (Neon Postgres) that holds your account, strategies, and access requests.
  • Resend: sends sign-in codes and service emails.
  • Vercel: hosts the stratBacktest app.
  • Cloudflare: runs the stratBacktest API and backtests.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.
  • FormSubmit: handles waitlist sign-ups.
  • Google: only if you choose Sign in with Google.

Backtests use market price data from a market data provider. We send it ticker symbols and dates, not your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and saved strategies while your account is open. We keep access requests (described above) until you ask us to delete them. Request logs are kept by our hosting providers for their standard periods.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account and everything we hold about you, including your saved strategies and access requests, by emailing tiltkillerapp@gmail.com. We'll do it within 30 days. You can also clear what's kept in your browser at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. If you signed in with Google, you can remove TiltKiller's access in your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Each user's strategies are kept separate in the database, so the app can only read the strategies of the signed-in user (and admins). No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

TiltKiller is not meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change matters to how we use your data, we'll tell signed-up users by email before it takes effect.
